How to Use This Editor

  1. Upload your photo by clicking the "Choose Image" button
  2. Select an editing tool from the options above
  3. Adjust the sliders to modify brightness, contrast, etc.
  4. Apply filters by clicking on the filter previews
  5. Click "Apply Changes" to see your edits
  6. Download your edited image when you're satisfied
